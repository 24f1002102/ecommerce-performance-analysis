## 1. Introduction

This project analyzes the quarterly customer retention rate for a major e-commerce business in 2024. The company has identified declining customer loyalty as a strategic concern, with retention significantly below the industry benchmark.

## 2. Dataset

| Quarter | Retention Rate |
|--------|----------------|
| Q1     | 68.58 |
| Q2     | 68.78 |
| Q3     | 70.29 |
| Q4     | 74.15 |

**Average: 70.45**

Industry target benchmark: **85**

## 3. Key Findings

1. Retention improved steadily over the year (increase of ~5.5 points from Q1 to Q4).
2. However, the business averaged **70.45**, significantly below the industry target of 85.
3. The gap between Q4 performance (74.15) and target remains high (~11 percentage points).
4. Improvement rate is gradual, but inadequate given competitive pressure.

### Visual Insights

The line chart (`retention_trend.png`) shows:

## 4. Business Implications

- Low retention increases **customer acquisition cost**, reducing profit margins.
- Repeat purchases drive **70-80% of revenue** in mature e-commerce ecosystems.
- Competitors with higher loyalty will scale faster and spend less on marketing.
- Without intervention, the company risks:
  - Lower CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)
  - Lower revenue predictability
  - Higher churn-driven volatility

## 5. Recommendations

To reach the benchmark of 85, the company should:

1. **Implement targeted retention campaigns** (core recommendation)
2. Personalize offers based on purchasing frequency and value
3. Deploy loyalty rewards for returning customers
4. Introduce post-purchase engagement workflows
5. Optimize customer experience on mobile platforms
6. Use predictive models to identify high risk churn segments
7. Improve delivery speed & return flexibility

## 6. Strategic Roadmap
